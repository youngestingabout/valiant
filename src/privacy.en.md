# Privacy Policy

Updated November 8, 2026

We keep little, we say what we keep, and we delete all of it when you ask. We do not sell your data, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

## 1. What we keep

- **A user id.** A random id from Google Firebase, so your progress follows you. Kept on your phone and our server until you delete your account.
- **Your settings and quiz answers.** Your topics, age band, minutes a day, reminder time, Bible version and language. Used to pick your plans and send your reminder. Kept on your phone and our server until you delete your account.
- **Your church background.** Kept only on your phone. It never leaves it.
- **Your reading progress and day count.** Kept on your phone and our server, so you can restore them on a new phone.
- **Bookmarks, text size and your last place in the Bible.** Kept only on your phone.
- **Your subscription.** Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases. We keep a copy of your plan and its dates on our server until you delete your account.
- **Apple Ads.** If you came from an Apple search ad, the campaign, ad group and keyword ids, so we know which searches bring readers. Kept until you delete your account.
- **App events.** Which screens you open and which steps you take, sent to PostHog with your user id. Never the words of your messages. Deleted with your account.
- **Crash reports.** Sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics without your user id, to fix crashes.
- **Sign in with Apple.** If you sign in, Google Firebase keeps your Apple user id and Apple's relay email address. Deleted with your account.

## 2. Your chat

- **Chat messages.** Sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write the answer. The chat is kept on your phone, never on our server. You can clear it at any time in the chat.
- **A chat log without the words.** The time, how long the answer took, its size and how many verses it cited. Kept on our server for 90 days, to watch cost and safety.
- **Your consent to the AI chat.** When you agreed and to which version of this policy. Kept until you delete your account.

## 3. Who receives data

- **Anthropic**, our AI provider, receives your chat messages to write the answer.
- **PostHog** receives app events, for our numbers.
- **RevenueCat and Apple** handle purchases.
- **Google Firebase** handles sign-in and crash reports.
- **Amazon Web Services** hosts our server, which also delivers the audio.

## 4. Deleting your data

In Valiant, go to You, then Delete my account and data. Your account is closed at once and this phone is wiped. Within 30 days we delete your account, your progress and your settings from our server, from PostHog, from Firebase and from RevenueCat. Apple keeps its own purchase records; cancel your subscription in the App Store or it keeps renewing.

## 5. Adults only

Valiant is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly keep data about anyone younger.

## 6. Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes. If the AI provider changes, the app asks for your consent again.
